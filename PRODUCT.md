# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

University and college students navigating mandatory lecture attendance rules (typically 75% or 80% attendance criteria) while balancing study, projects, exam schedules, and personal freedom.

## Product Purpose

Skiply calculates the exact margin of classes a student can safely skip without dropping below their required semester attendance threshold. It converts tedious daily manual record-keeping into single-tap attendance logging and automated timetable extraction.

## Positioning

Unlike conventional attendance trackers that merely display raw attendance counts or percentages, Skiply is an intelligent forward-looking safe margin engine: it calculates the mathematically verified number of safe skips remaining across each individual course and lab before every single class.

## Operating Context

- Mobile web & Progressive Web App (PWA) accessed primarily on mobile smartphones (360px–390px) between classes.
- Used in low-connectivity lecture halls with offline-first local queue sync.
- Timetable input via camera photo/screenshot upload with AI optical course extraction.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-tap daily attendance marking (Present, Absent, Cancelled).
- Automated AI timetable extraction from PNG/JPG schedule images.
- Precise safe-skip margin calculator with tabular percentage telemetry.
- Strict low-end hardware tier with zero-GPU fallbacks and flat near-black rendering.
- RFC 5545 `.ics` timetable export for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Outlook.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Skiply
- **Voice**: Quiet, editorial, precise, confident.
- **Logomark**: The Minimal Forward Skip — an architectural kinetic 'S' with a 45° diagonal notch cut and a single teal-400 threshold pivot point.
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Flat near-black (`#0B0C0E`), Instrument Serif display headings, Plus Jakarta Sans UI, and disciplined teal-400 accent reserved strictly for the primary action and safe margin state.

## Product Principles

1. **Safety Margin Clarity**: Answer "Can I safely skip today?" in under 2 seconds without mental math.
2. **Frictionless Entry**: Single-tap daily logging; offline-first resilient storage.
3. **Restrained Editorial Craft**: Zero decorative gradients, no AI slop, no visual noise.
4. **Student Privacy**: Zero telemetry tracking, client-side guest storage, transparent academic schedule exports.
