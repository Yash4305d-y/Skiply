1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL UPGRADES
================================================================================
Skiply has been upgraded to provide a more granular, intuitive, and frictionless
attendance planning experience for college students. 

Key Upgrades in this Specification:
1. Subject-Wise Safe Skips & Math Engine: Attendance percentages and safe skip
   limits are no longer just global. Colleges enforce attendance *per subject*.
   The engine now calculates exact percentages, safe skip buffers, and recovery
   requirements for EACH subject individually.
2. Visual Calendar History View: A dedicated calendar interface where students
   can visually audit their semester. Days and specific lecture slots are color-coded
   (Green = Present, Red = Absent, Gray = Cancelled, Blue = Upcoming).
3. Simplified "Holiday List" Onboarding: Replacing the complex Academic Calendar
   upload with a lightweight "Holiday List" upload/input. Students simply upload
   an image of their holiday list or paste the dates, which the AI parses instantly.

3. STREAMLINED ONBOARDING WORKFLOW (AI PARSER)
================================================================================
The onboarding pipeline is reduced to two simple steps:

[Step 1: Class Timetable Upload]
- User uploads an image/screenshot or PDF of their weekly class timetable.
- AI extracts: Subject Names, Subject Codes, Days of the Week, Start/End Times,
  and Room Numbers.
- User reviews and confirms the extracted grid in an interactive editable UI.

[Step 2: Holiday List Upload / Entry]
- Instead of a complex multi-page academic calendar, the user uploads a simple
  image/PDF of the "Semester Holiday List" OR pastes text directly into a text area.
- AI extracts: Holiday Date (YYYY-MM-DD) and Holiday Name (e.g., "Diwali", "Labor Day").
- The system asks for the Semester Start Date and Semester End Date via two simple
  date pickers to establish the semester boundary.

5. CORE ATTENDANCE CALCULATION LOGIC & SUBJECT-WISE MATH ENGINE
================================================================================
The attendance percentage is ALWAYS calculated based on the number of classes that
should have been conducted up to the current date (Conducted Till Date), NOT the 
total classes in the entire semester.

[Dynamic Conducted Till Date Calculation]
Every day, the app automatically calculates how many lectures should have been 
conducted for each subject from the semester start date until today by:
1. Reading the weekly timetable.
2. Counting only the scheduled lecture days.
3. Ignoring dates present in the academic holidays list.
4. Adding extra classes and removing cancelled classes if applicable.

[Subject-Wise Formulas]
1. Current Attendance Percentage (Present-Day Accuracy):
   Attendance % = (Classes Attended / Classes Conducted Till Date) * 100
   * This accurately reflects the student's real-time attendance status as used by colleges.

2. Semester Total Class Available:
   Total Available = Conducted Till Date + Remaining Scheduled Lectures
   * Remaining lectures are calculated from tomorrow until semester_end_date (excluding weekends without classes and holidays).

3. Required Presents in Semester (To Reach Target % e.g. 75% or 80%):
   Required Total Presents = ceil( (Target % / 100.0) * Total Available )

4. Safe Skips Available (When Current % >= Target %):
   Safe Skips (S) = floor( (Attended / (Target % / 100.0)) - Total Available )
   * Represents how many consecutive upcoming lectures can be skipped without dropping below the required target percentage.

5. Recovery Classes Needed (Danger Zone: When Attended < Required Total Presents):
   Must Attend (N) = Required Total Presents - Attended
   * Out of the fixed semester Total Available classes, this is the exact number of additional classes the student must attend to reach their target percentage.
